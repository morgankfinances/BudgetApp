Coinrose is a household budgeting app run by Morgan Keller ("I," "me"). It's a personal project, not a bank or other financial institution. This policy explains what information Coinrose handles, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

The short version:

  • You add your financial information yourself by uploading statement files. Coinrose never asks for or stores your bank logins, and never connects to your bank.
  • Your household's data is visible to its members, and to no other user of the app.
  • Your data isn't sold, rented, or used for advertising, and Coinrose shows no ads.
  • As the person who runs the app, I have administrative access to the database. I don't look at your data, but that access exists.
  • You can download all of your data, or delete your account, at any time from the app.

What information Coinrose collects

Your account

Your email address, and a password if you choose to set one. Passwords are never stored in a readable form: they're stored only as a scrambled one-way "hash" by the sign-in service (Supabase), which can check a password without keeping it. Records of when you signed up and signed in are also kept.

Your household

Your household's name, who its members are (their email addresses and whether each is an owner or a member), its invite code, and any requests to join it.

The financial information you add

Everything you put into Coinrose: account names, transactions (date, description, and amounts), categories, budgets, savings goals, budget groups, and settings like your planned income.

When you upload a statement file from your bank, Coinrose keeps only the columns you choose to use (the date, description, and amount columns, plus the bank's reference ID for each transaction if the file has one: a number identifying a single transaction, never an account number). Any other columns in the file, like account numbers, card numbers, memos, or running balances, are discarded before anything is saved.

Data History

To let you undo mistakes, Coinrose keeps earlier versions of changed items for 7 days. After that they're deleted automatically.

Settings kept only on your device

Some preferences are stored only in your browser and never sent to Coinrose: your chosen color theme, your Reports settings, and whether you've seen the welcome tour and the "Before you get started" notice. The sign-in session that keeps you signed in is also stored in your browser. Coinrose doesn't use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Technical information

  • Bot protection. The sign-in page uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from automated bots. It examines signals from your browser and device to make that decision, usually without you noticing.
  • Crash reports. If part of the app crashes, a report goes to Sentry so the problem can be fixed. Reports include the error, your browser and operating system, and the page you were on. They're set up not to include your name, email address, [IP address — confirm "Prevent Storing of IP Addresses" is on in Sentry], what you clicked, or any of your financial information. Email addresses and long numbers are removed from error messages before they're sent.
  • Service logs. Like most websites, the companies that host Coinrose automatically keep short-term logs of requests (such as the time, the page requested, and the requesting IP address) for security and reliability.

What Coinrose doesn't collect

  • Bank, card, or other financial account logins
  • Full account or card numbers (see "The financial information you add" above)
  • Advertising or tracking data; there are no ads, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics

How your information is used

Only to provide Coinrose: to sign you in, store and show your household's data, share it with your household's members, send sign-in and password-reset emails, keep the service secure, and fix problems. Your information isn't sold, rented, shared for advertising, or used to build a profile of you.

Who can see your information

Your household

Everyone in your household can see all of its financial data and each member's email address and role. Owners can approve new members. Keep that in mind before inviting someone: they'll see everything in the household.

If someone leaves or is removed from a household, they normally keep a copy of the household's data up to that point, in a household of their own. When removing someone, an owner can instead choose to remove them without a copy.

Other users

No one outside your household can see your household's data. This separation is enforced by the database itself, not just by what the app displays.

The operator

As the person running Coinrose, I have administrative access to the database where everything is stored, as the operator of any hosted app does. I don't review your data. I'd only access it when needed to keep the service working (for example, to fix a problem you've reported), to protect the service or its users, or if required by law.

When required by law

Information may be disclosed if required by law, such as a valid court order.

How long information is kept

  • Your account and household data: until you delete them.
  • Data History: 7 days.
  • After you delete your account: your login is deleted immediately. If you were your household's only member, the household and all of its data are deleted too. If others share the household, its data stays with them. Deleted data may remain in the database provider's backups for up to 7 days before it's gone for good. Though, there may be some changes around this due to free vs paid tiers of Supabase.
  • Crash reports and service logs: kept for the provider's standard period, then deleted.

Your choices

  • See and download your data: everything is visible in the app, and the Backup page downloads it as spreadsheet files.
  • Correct it: edit any transaction, category, or setting in the app.
  • Delete it: use "Delete my account" in Settings, or email me.
  • Leave a household: "Leave this household" in Settings. You keep a copy of its data up to that point.
  • Questions or requests: email morgankfinances@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, such as the right to request a copy of your personal information. Email me and I'll help.

Security

Coinrose uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) everywhere, stores data with a provider that encrypts it at rest, keeps each household's data separate at the database level, and protects sign-in against automated attacks. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password, and keep a backup of anything important.

Children

Coinrose is meant for adults and isn't directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, email me and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. For significant changes, you'll also be told in the app or by email before they take effect.
